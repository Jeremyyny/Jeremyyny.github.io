I am an Applied Scientist at Amazon working on large-scale AI memory and self-improving personalization systems. My work focuses on customer modeling and on capturing, structuring, retrieving, and reasoning over heterogeneous memory sources, behavioral signals, and multimodal interaction histories to build context-aware, adaptive, and recursive self-evolving personalized AI memory systems that learn from users over time and support personalized recommendations, interactions, and agentic experiences.

My research interests span LLM reasoning and post-training, reinforcement learning, multi-agent systems, long-horizon agentic systems, memory and personalization, recursive self-improving AI, and AI4Science. I am particularly interested in agentic systems that can reason, retrieve knowledge, use tools, collaborate with other agents, and learn from experience through memory and feedback loops, as well as their applications to scientific reasoning and discovery.

Before joining Amazon, I developed AI tools to support scientific reviewers at the [U.S. Food and Drug Administration](https://www.fda.gov/). I worked with [Qi Liu](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=LVO1_ZsAAAAJ&hl=en), [Hadi Kharrazi](http://hkharrazi.com/), and [David C. Fajgenbaum](https://www.med.upenn.edu/apps/faculty/index.php/g275/p8205911) at the Center for Drug Evaluation and Research. My academic collaborators have included [Yuzhe Yang](https://people.csail.mit.edu/yuzhe/) and [Ying Nian Wu](http://www.stat.ucla.edu/~ywu/).

Before joining the FDA, I earned a master’s degree in computer science with a concentration in AI from the [University of California, Los Angeles](https://www.ucla.edu/) and a bachelor’s degree in computer science and quantitative economics from the [University of California, Irvine](https://uci.edu/). I was also fortunate to be advised by [Amir M. Rahmani](https://ics.uci.edu/~amirr1/) at the [UCI HealthSciTech Group](https://healthscitech.nursing.uci.edu/), and [Guang Cheng](http://www.stat.ucla.edu/~guangcheng/2.html) at UCLA, where I researched synthetic data through deep generative modeling.

**My broader research goal is to build adaptive, reliable, personalized, and self-improving AI systems that can continuously learn from interactions and experience at scale.**
